Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

The short version: TaxDeduct has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device. We collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. TaxDeduct does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

Where your data lives

Expenses, mileage, notes, receipt photos and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Receipt photos

Photos you attach are stored on your device only and are never uploaded.

Location

If you turn on automatic mileage, TaxDeduct uses your location — including while the app is in the background — to detect when you are driving.

  • Location is processed on your device to compute trip distance.
  • Only the resulting distance in miles is saved, as a trip entry.
  • Raw coordinates are never stored and never transmitted.
  • You can turn this off in Settings, or revoke access in iOS Settings.

Backups

Encrypted backups are created on your device using a passphrase you choose (AES-GCM with PBKDF2-SHA256 key derivation). Where the file goes is up to you. We never see it and cannot recover it if you lose the passphrase.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple's handling of that data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third parties

TaxDeduct contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children

TaxDeduct is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.

Contact

Questions: support@mealpal.cn

TaxDeduct is a record-keeping tool. It does not file taxes and does not provide tax advice.
