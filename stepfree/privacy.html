Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

StepFree is a habit-tracking app developed by ZHAOQUANHONG ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains what data the app handles and how. We designed StepFree to be private by default: your habits and history belong to you, not to us.

1. Data we do not collect

We do not operate any servers that store your data. We do not require an account, do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information, and do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We have no way to read, access, or sell your habit data.

2. Where your data is stored

Your habits, records, and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. If you are signed in to iCloud and have iCloud Drive enabled, your data may also be synced through Apple's CloudKit to your own private iCloud database, so it stays in sync across your devices.

This iCloud sync is provided by Apple and is bound to your private iCloud account. We cannot access it. You can disable it at any time by turning off iCloud Drive for StepFree in your device Settings.

3. Voice input

When you use the voice-add feature, your speech is processed by Apple's speech recognition service (SFSpeechRecognizer). Depending on your device, recognition may run on-device or through Apple's servers. Apple's handling of this data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store your audio.

The app requires your permission to access the microphone and speech recognition. You can revoke these permissions in Settings at any time.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment information. Transaction and subscription records are managed by Apple and can be viewed in your Apple ID account.

5. Notifications

If you set a reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. These notifications are delivered by iOS and are not sent through any of our servers. You can disable them in Settings at any time.

6. Permissions used

  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — used only for the voice-add feature.
  • Notifications — used only for reminders you choose to set.

7. Data retention and deletion

Deleting a habit removes it and its history from your device. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data. To remove any data synced to iCloud, you can delete StepFree's data from iCloud in your device Settings, or contact us for guidance.

8. Children's privacy

StepFree is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at support@mealpal.cn.
