Frequently asked questions about StepFree.

Does StepFree sync across my devices?

Yes. If you're signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive enabled, your habits sync across your devices through your private iCloud account. We cannot access your data.

Why can't I start voice input?

Voice input needs two things: permission to use the microphone and speech recognition, and (for some devices) an internet connection. Check Settings > Privacy to make sure both permissions are granted, and confirm you're online.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchase. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so use the same account you bought with.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free lets you track up to 3 habits and try voice-add once. Pro unlocks unlimited habits and unlimited voice-add with a one-time purchase — no subscription.

Why didn't I get a reminder?

Reminders are local notifications. Make sure notifications are allowed for StepFree in Settings, and that you've set a reminder time on the habit.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a habit from its detail menu to remove it and its history. To remove everything, delete the app. For iCloud data, you can remove StepFree's data in Settings > iCloud > Manage Storage.
