Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add an RSU grant?

Tap the "+" button in the top-right corner and choose "Add RSU Grant". Enter your grant name, ticker symbol, total shares, vesting start date, cliff period, and number of batches. Granted calculates the full vesting schedule for you.

How is my vesting schedule calculated?

Granted starts from your vesting start date, applies your cliff period for the first batch, then spaces the remaining batches evenly according to your vesting frequency. The final batch is adjusted so the total shares always add up exactly to your grant total.

How is the tax estimate calculated?

The after-tax estimate uses a standard 22% federal withholding, your selected state rate, and 1.45% Medicare, then applies sell-to-cover to show the shares and cash you keep after taxes. It is an estimate for planning only — consult a tax professional for your specific situation.

How do I enable Face ID lock?

Open Settings within the app, turn on "Require Face ID", and confirm. The app will prompt for authentication on launch and whenever it returns from the background. Your biometric data never leaves your device.

How do I change vesting reminders?

In Settings you can choose how many days before a vest the reminder fires, and at what hour. Reminders are delivered as local notifications on your device.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual grants and ESPP periods from within the app. To remove everything at once, uninstall the app — all data is stored only on your device and is erased on deletion.
