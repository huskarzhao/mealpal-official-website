Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Granted ("the App") is developed and operated by ZHAOQUANHONG ("we", "our"). This policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

In short: Granted is a privacy-first app. We do not collect, store, or sell any of your personal data. All of your equity information stays on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies of any kind.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All data you enter — RSU grants, ESPP periods, vesting schedules, and app settings — is stored only on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData and UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device and is never accessible to us.

3. Stock Quotes

The App retrieves live stock prices from a third-party market data provider (Yahoo Finance). When it does so, only the stock ticker symbol you have entered (for example, "AAPL") is sent in the request. No personal or identifying information is transmitted.

4. Face ID

The optional Face ID lock uses Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. Biometric authentication is performed entirely on your device by Apple; your biometric data is never stored, accessed, or transmitted by us.

5. Notifications

Vesting reminders are delivered as local notifications generated on your device. No push-notification service or server is involved.

6. In-App Purchases

Purchases (such as Granted Pro) are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

7. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose your information to any third party, because we do not have it.

8. Data Deletion

Because all data is stored on your device, deleting the App removes all of your equity data permanently.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at support@mealpal.cn.
