Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data Word Sleuth collects and how it is used.

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Who we are

Word Sleuth is published by Beijing Fandazi Network Technology Co., Ltd. ("we", "us").

Data we collect

Word Sleuth is an offline game. We do not require an account, and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos.

Your game progress, settings, coins, and preferences are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Third-party advertising (Google AdMob)

Word Sleuth displays ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure these ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect certain non-personally-identifying data, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier)
  • IP address (used to estimate coarse, city-level location)
  • Ad interaction and usage data
  • Diagnostic data (crash logs, performance data)

We serve non-personalized ads: we do not use your advertising identifier to track you across apps and websites, and we do not combine the above data with any personal information we hold (we hold none).

Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn more about how Google uses data from partner apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

All in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or see your payment details (such as credit card information). Apple's own privacy practices apply to payment and billing.

Game Center

If you use Game Center leaderboards, your leaderboard score and display name are handled by Apple according to Apple's privacy policy. We do not store this data ourselves.

Children

Word Sleuth is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal information ourselves, there is typically no personal data for us to provide, correct, or delete. If you have a question or request regarding your privacy, please contact us and we will respond promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
