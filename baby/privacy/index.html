隐私政策

我们始终坚持最少收集、透明使用、绝不售卖的原则。

更新日期：2026-10-19

简介

「拾光育儿」（BabyJournal）由北京饭搭子网络科技有限公司开发。我们深知宝宝信息的敏感性，本隐私政策说明我们如何处理你的信息。我们始终坚持最少收集、透明使用、绝不售卖的原则。

我们收集的信息

  • 宝宝资料：你主动填写的宝宝昵称、生日、性别等基础信息。
  • 成长记录：你记录的喂养、睡眠、换尿布、成长里程碑等文字内容，以及你选择添加的照片。
  • 设备信息：仅用于正常运行的设备型号、系统版本等基础信息。

信息如何使用

用于生成你的宝宝成长档案、发育评估和日常小结。

AI 服务说明

当你使用 AI 解读、发育评估、今日小结等功能时，相关的文字记录会发送至我们的 AI 服务提供商（DeepSeek）进行处理，以生成专业的育儿解读。我们仅传输生成解读所必需的内容，AI 服务商不会将你的数据用于其他用途。

数据存储

数据仅存储在本地设备，你可以离线使用，我们不会将你的数据上传到任何云端服务器。

第三方服务

  • Apple：提供应用内购买（订阅）服务。
  • DeepSeek：提供 AI 文本生成服务。
  • 腾讯云（Tencent Cloud）：提供 AI 请求的代理中转服务。

订阅与支付

订阅通过 Apple 的应用内购买完成。所有支付信息由 Apple 直接处理，我们无法获取、也不会存储你的银行卡或支付信息。

儿童隐私

本应用由家长或监护人使用，用于记录宝宝信息。所有宝宝信息均由家长主动输入，我们不会向儿童直接收集任何信息。

数据删除

你可以随时在应用内删除任意记录。卸载应用会删除本地数据。

联系我们

如有任何隐私问题，请联系：support@fandazi.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Introduction

"BabyJournal" is developed by Beijing Fandazi Network Technology Co., Ltd. We understand the sensitivity of baby information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information. We always follow the principles of minimal collection, transparent use, and never selling your data.

Information We Collect

  • Baby profile: nickname, birthday, gender, and other basic information you voluntarily provide.
  • Growth records: feeding, sleep, diaper, milestone text records, and photos you choose to add.
  • Device information: basic information such as device model and system version, used only for normal operation.

How We Use Information

To generate your baby's growth archive, development assessments, and daily summaries.

AI Service

When you use AI interpretation, development assessment, or daily summary features, relevant text records are sent to our AI service provider (DeepSeek) for processing to generate professional parenting insights. We only transmit content necessary for generating insights, and the AI provider will not use your data for any other purpose.

Data Storage

Data is stored locally on your device only; you can use it offline. We do not upload your data to any cloud server.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple: provides in-app purchase (subscription) services.
  • DeepSeek: provides AI text generation services.
  • Tencent Cloud: provides proxy relay for AI requests.

Subscription & Payment

Subscriptions are completed through Apple's in-app purchase. All payment information is handled directly by Apple. We cannot access or store your payment information.

Children's Privacy

This app is used by parents or guardians to record baby information. All baby information is entered by parents voluntarily. We do not collect any information directly from children.

Data Deletion

You can delete any record at any time within the app. Uninstalling the app deletes local data.

Contact Us

For any privacy questions, please contact: support@fandazi.com
