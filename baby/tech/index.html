常见问题

如何恢复订阅？

在会员页点击「恢复购买」，系统会通过 Apple ID 自动恢复你的订阅。

如何取消订阅？

打开 iOS「设置」→ 点击你的 Apple ID →「订阅」→ 选择「拾光育儿」→ 取消订阅。取消后当前周期内仍可继续使用。

我的数据存在哪里？

数据仅存储在本地设备，不会上传到任何云端服务器。卸载应用会删除本地数据。

AI 解读是医疗建议吗？

不是。AI 解读仅为育儿参考，不构成任何医疗诊断或治疗建议。如有健康问题，请咨询专业医生。

如何删除我的数据？

在应用内直接删除记录即可；卸载应用会清除本地数据。

Tech Support

FAQ

How do I restore my subscription?

Tap "Restore Purchases" on the membership page. Your subscription will be restored automatically via your Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iOS Settings → tap your Apple ID → Subscriptions → select "BabyJournal" → Cancel Subscription. You can continue using it until the current period ends.

Where is my data stored?

Data is stored locally on your device only and is not uploaded to any cloud server. Uninstalling the app deletes local data.

Is the AI interpretation medical advice?

No. AI interpretation is for parenting reference only and does not constitute medical diagnosis or treatment advice. Please consult a professional doctor for health concerns.

How do I delete my data?

Delete records directly within the app. Uninstalling the app clears local data.
